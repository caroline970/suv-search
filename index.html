  • Full-size body / real usable 3rd row — non-negotiable

  • Better gas mileage preferred · delivery anywhere fine

Recommendation

With diesel ruled out, no full-size SUV gets good gas mileage — they're all ~14–16 mpg. So it comes down to two choices:

  • Path A — full-size 3rd row + captain's: your captain's-verified value picks are the Infiniti QX80 ($43k) and Jeep Wagoneer ($45k); step up to Escalade ($59k), Yukon Denali ($65k), or Sequoia Platinum ($68k) for presence/reliability. All ~14–16 mpg.
  • Path B — accept a slightly smaller 3rd row for real efficiency: the Grand Highlander Hybrid ($35k, ~30 mpg) or Lincoln Aviator ($28k) — both captain's-verified, far cheaper, far thriftier. Usable adult 3rd row, just not the full-size cavern.

My take: if full-size is the hard line, the QX80 or Wagoneer give you captain's chairs + presence for ~$43–45k. If you can live with a slightly smaller 3rd row, the Grand Highlander Hybrid is the smart-money pick — captain's, ~30 mpg, and half the fuel cost of the big trucks.
