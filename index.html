The call — my ranking & why

Scored for a 10-year keeper · captain's chairs · a genuinely usable 3rd row · a cool interior · black/navy · sensible cost. The top two are the real decision; 3–8 are strong alternatives.

  1. Cadillac Escalade — the safe 10-year keeper and the flashiest cabin. Biggest all-rounder; the catch is fuel + price.
  2. Cadillac Vistiq (electric) — nearly as cool, cheapest to run, newest tech, charging-ready garage. Catch: first-year EV, battery ages over a decade.
  3. Toyota Sequoia Platinum — if "will it last 10 years?" is the deciding question, this is the bulletproof full-size.
  4. Jeep Wagoneer — the most 3rd-row space for the least money; buy Certified to cover the so-so reliability.
  5. GMC Yukon Denali — handsome full-size luxe, but pricey and thirsty.
  6. Lexus TX — refined and Lexus-durable, just a size down and understated.
  7. Toyota Grand Highlander Hybrid — the sensible-money pick: reliable, ~30 mpg, cheap — smaller 3rd row, plainer cabin.
  8. Lincoln Aviator — plush cabin for the least money; tightest 3rd row + average reliability.

Bottom line: it's Escalade vs. Vistiq — the proven, flashiest 10-year flagship, or the cool electric Cadillac that costs a fraction to run. Everything else is a value, space, or reliability variation.
